# Sailing towards smoother banking with user-friendly products and design thinking.

## | User research | Design evangelism | UX Design | Design Thinking | Interaction Design | Credit lifecycle | Agile |

I have been fortunate to spend the past five months working as part of Credit Suisse's Digital Transformation and Product Labs team. In this role, I have been able to help conduct user research and redesign the user experience of credit lifecycle products for the banking industry.

Currently, I am focused on improving the internal design thinking workshops, with the goal of accelerating Credit Suisse's shift towards a more agile and user-centered approach.

**Delivery** Currently building products to Optimize and monitor Credit facility lifecycle.

**Role** Design Intern within the Department of Design and Discovery - Digital Transformation Product Labs, Credit Suisse.

**Timeline** Sept 2022 - Present
